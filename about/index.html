02 / How I work

Sketch first.
Sometimes.

Sketch first, or straight to CAD? There isn’t a single right answer. For me sketching is usually where the early thinking happens — fast, rough, low commitment. That said, I go straight into 3D sometimes too, when I already know the form and just want to get it out before the idea moves.

  1. Foundation

    Sketch plane, centreline, construction line. A robust sketch is built, not drawn — and this is where every model begins.

  2. Generation

    Line, arc, circle, ellipse, spline, polygon. Getting the volume and proportion out of the head and onto the screen without over-committing.

  3. Modification

    Offset, trim, extend, mirror, pattern. Refining the form against real constraints — manufacturing, packaging, assembly.

  4. Control

    Constraint and dimension. Locking the design down so it can be manufactured, costed and produced reliably at scale.
