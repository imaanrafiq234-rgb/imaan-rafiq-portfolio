02 / How I work

Sketch first. Sometimes.

Sketch first, or straight to CAD? I don’t think there’s a single right answer. For me sketching is usually where the early thinking happens — fast, rough, low commitment. That said, I go straight into 3D sometimes too, when I already know the form and just want to get it out before the idea moves.

Foundation

Sketch plane, centreline, construction line

A robust sketch is built, not drawn. It follows a strict hierarchy of operations — and this is where every model begins.

Generation

Line, arc, circle, ellipse, spline, polygon

Getting the volume and proportion out of the head and onto the screen without over-committing.

Modification

Offset, trim, extend, mirror, pattern

Refining the form against real constraints — manufacturing, packaging, assembly.

Control

Constraint and dimension

Locking the design down so it can be manufactured, costed and produced reliably at scale.
