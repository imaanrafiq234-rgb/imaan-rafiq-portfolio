    • Great automotive design isn't drawn. It's calculated. Behind every icon like the Audi R8 is a constant battle between styling and physics.

    Proportional stance

    A mid-engine layout forces a cab-forward volume to perfectly balance axle weight. The proportion is a consequence of where the mass has to sit.

    Lighting architecture

    Sharp DRL matrices define brand identity while framing front cooling ducts. Lighting does aerodynamic work as well as brand work.

    Aerodynamic taper

    A Coke-bottle side profile channels clean airflow straight to the rear wing.

    Surface tension

    Concave flanks pull the bodywork tight to scoop high-pressure air into the side blades. Form never merely follows function. Form is function.
