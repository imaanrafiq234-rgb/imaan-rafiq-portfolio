    • Revisiting my very first university design project has been a valuable reminder of how far my skills and design thinking have progressed — and how much my early designs relied on enthusiasm rather than refinement.

    The brief focused on designing a rugged speaker, requiring a balance between durability, functionality and visual appeal. The process reinforced the importance of research, data collection and form analysis in guiding design decisions.

    Process

    I explored different shapes and materials, then iterated the design to improve the clay model, the internal layout, the render and the overall form. Initial sketches drove the shape; the physical model validated how it sat in the hand.

    Accessibility and protection

    Port placement was a deliberate decision: making the ports accessible and obtainable while reducing the risk of the speaker getting damaged. The final design presents the USB ports and charging port clearly on the body.

    Reflection

    Alongside traditional design methods, AI has since become a valuable tool in my workflow — supporting ideation, exploration and efficiency while keeping human creativity and judgement at the core.

    Looking back: a sleek, modern form; compact and portable; designed for everyday use; a strong aesthetic focus, though the technical detailing could go further. This project is where I developed form and styling skills.
