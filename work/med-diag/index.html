    • Field triage is a one-handed job. Med-Diag X1 is a handheld biometric platform built for a clinician who is holding a patient, a torch and a deadline at once.

    Non-invasive sensing

    PPG optical pulse monitoring and contactless infrared thermopiles let the device read vitals without breaking skin, which matters enormously when a patient has to be assessed quickly in the field.

    Product realisation

    A sanitary matte white antimicrobial casing carries a high-contrast clinical OLED interface, framed to stay legible in daylight and easy to wipe down between patients.

    Anthropometric study

    The thumb-wheel control was placed by calculating the single-handed thumb sweep across the 5th to 95th percentile hand reach, with internal thermal cutaways keeping the electronics within safe limits.
