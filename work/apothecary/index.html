    • A packaging identity built around the language of a limited apothecary batch: batch numbers, cold-pressed claims and a ritual you claim rather than buy.

    Structure and volumetrics

    The primary carton is developed as a structure and volumetrics study, resolving how much air the pack can afford to hold before it reads as waste on a shelf.

    Texture and swatch reference

    A swatch reference fixes the surface finish, ink behaviour and the uncoated stock feel. Formulated to restore vital cellular lipid barriers with nutrient-dense active botanicals, the copy stays clinical while the palette stays warm.

    The ritual

    A complementary pouch and batch card carry the cold-pressed claim and a drop of the seed oil itself, turning the unboxing into the first step of the routine rather than an afterthought.
