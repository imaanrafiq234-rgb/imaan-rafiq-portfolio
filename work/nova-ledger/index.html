    • I spent a weekend playing around with layout, typography, and dark-mode UI. I wanted to challenge myself to take a screen usually crammed with numbers and make it feel clean, calm, and actually pleasant to look at.

    So I put together a concept advertisement carousel for a project I named NOVA Ledger. Instead of focusing on the tech behind it, I used this as a playground to sharpen my visual design skills.

    Creating room to breathe

    Stacking the headline and body text on the left while framing the visual cards on the right gives your eyes a clear, comfortable path to follow. The asymmetry is deliberate: text anchors, product floats.

    Typographic hierarchy

    Pairing a super bold display face with a simple body font makes sure your brain instantly knows where to look first. Headlines do the shouting; the supporting copy stays quiet and legible.

    Colour logic

    I went with a deep forest-green canvas to keep things easy on the eyes, saving bright lime green only for the metrics and buttons that need to stand out. The accent becomes a signal rather than decoration.

    Subtle depth

    Soft drop shadows, card overlays and rounded corners make the floating UI elements feel tactile and modern — the interface reads as physical objects resting on a surface rather than flat rectangles.
