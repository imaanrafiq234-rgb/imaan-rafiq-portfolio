    • The brief was to design a future robot to solve a problem or assist with a task, practising surface modelling and model quality.

    Here are a few initial iterations for an underwater drone design, heavily inspired by the Golden Snitch from Harry Potter. Nothing has to be perfect at this stage — just diving into some raw sketches and early ideation.

    Research and insight

    The concept was grounded in drowning and water safety, regenerative design, existing drones on the market, and a form analysis of the golden snitch silhouette — reconciling a playful form language with a serious safety function.

    Process and outcome

    From research through storyboarding to surface modelling, the project was an exercise in form quality: how closely can an organic, spherical form be controlled in CAD without losing the original sketch intent.
