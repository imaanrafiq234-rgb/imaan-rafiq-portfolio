02 / Approach

Form never merely follows function. Form is function.

01

Understand the form before the software

How often do we jump straight into CAD before truly understanding a form? The strongest designs start with a breakdown of shape, proportion and volume — on paper, quickly, without commitment.

02

Make it physical early

Clay, card and 3D-printed models fix ergonomics and apply the user journey in a way a screen cannot. Without that step there is no kaizen.

03

Engineer it honestly

Internal layout, tolerances, sensor packaging, BOM costing and FEA are the parts of design that deserve far more recognition than the exterior silhouette. Functionality should never compromise form — and form should never compromise the person using it.

04

Design for everyone who needs it

Mobility issues do not discriminate by income. Assistive technology split into affordable and premium tiers is a design decision as much as an ethical one.

03 / The practice

“Professional UX isn’t about perfect starting points, it’s about intentional user flows.”

I sketch two to three times a week and post the results, because sketching still gets undervalued in design conversations that jump straight to renders. It is where the early thinking happens — fast, rough, low commitment.
